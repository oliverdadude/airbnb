# Outreach Scripts

Copy, personalise, send. Keep it short and friendly.

---

## 1. Talking to your cleaners (in person is best)

> Hey [name], I'm starting a business cleaning Airbnbs between guests and I'd love you to be part of it. I'd find the clients and handle all the booking and billing, and you'd just do the cleans. It'd be £[40] for a 2-bed, about 2.5 hours, paid every Friday. Most cleans are between 11 and 4, a lot of them on weekends. You can say yes or no to any job. Would that interest you?

Questions to ask them:
- Can you do weekends? Which days are you definitely free?
- How long would a 2-bed Airbnb take you?
- Does that pay feel fair compared to what you earn now?
- Do you know anyone else good who might want extra work?

---

## 2. Friends & family

> Hi! I've just started **FreshTurn**, a cleaning service for Airbnb hosts. We clean between guests, change the beds, restock, and send photos after every clean. Do you know anyone in Bath who rents out a place on Airbnb or as a holiday let? I'm giving the first clean at 50% off. Would really appreciate an intro 🙏 [website link]

---

## 3. Facebook host group post

*(Read the group rules first. Some only allow promotion on certain days.)*

> 👋 Hi hosts! I run **FreshTurn**, a local Bath team that does Airbnb and holiday let changeovers.
>
> ✅ Synced to your Airbnb calendar, so cleans get booked automatically
> ✅ Photo report after every clean (handy for damage claims)
> ✅ Weekends and same-day turnovers
> ✅ Backup cleaner always available, so no missed cleans
>
> 2-bed from £60. **First clean 50% off** for anyone in this group. Message me or get an instant price here: [website link]

Also: answer other people's cleaning questions helpfully without pitching. That builds trust faster than ads.

---

We handle changeovers between guests, with a photo report every time and backup cleaners so nothing's ever missed. If you're ever short on cleaning help, I'd love to do a first clean at half price so you can see the quality. No pressure at all! [phone / website]

> Hi [name], thanks so much for trying FreshTurn! Hope the place looked great for your guests. If you're happy, a quick Google review would mean a lot to a small business like ours: [Google review link]. And if you know any other hosts, we'll give you a free clean for every one who signs up 😊
