# Message Templates

Copy, fill in the [brackets], send. Use WhatsApp Business so work chats stay separate.

---

## To hosts

**After a clean**
> Hi [name], [property] is all done and ready for [time] check-in ✅ Photos here: [link]. [Any notes, e.g. "Restocked toilet roll and coffee." / "Nothing to report."]

**Low stock (if the host buys supplies)**
> Hi [name], quick heads-up: [property] is running low on [items]. There's enough for [1–2] more stays. Want me to pick some up and add it to this month's invoice, or will you top it up?

**Replacement over £20, needs approval**
> Hi [name], [item] at [property] is [broken / missing / worn out] (photo attached). A replacement is about £[price] from [shop]. OK for me to buy it and add it to your invoice?

**Guest damage**
> Hi [name], we found some damage at [property] after the [date] checkout: [what]. Photos and timestamps here: [link]. If you'd like to claim it from the guest through [Airbnb / Booking.com], the deadline is **[checkout date + 14 days]**. I [have replaced / can replace] [item] for £[cost]. Let me know if you need anything else for the claim.

**Running late**
> Hi [name], we're running about [X] mins behind at [property] because [reason]. We'll still be finished by [time]. I'll message as soon as it's ready.

**Guest still in the property**
> Hi [name], [cleaner] arrived at [property] at [time] but the guest is still inside. Could you check with them? We'll wait until [time], then [come back at X / mark it as a late cancellation].

**Monthly invoice**
> Hi [name], here's your invoice for [month]: [X] cleans plus £[Y] of supplies and replacements (receipts attached). Total £[total], due by [date]. Bank details are on the invoice. Thanks so much!

**Payment reminder (polite)**
> Hi [name], just a reminder that invoice [FT-000X] for £[total] was due on [date]. Could you take a look when you get a sec? Thanks!

**Asking for a review**
> Hi [name], glad the first month went well! If you're happy with us, a quick Google review would really help a small local business: [link]. And if you know other hosts in Bath, we'll give you a free clean for every one who signs up 😊

## To cleaners

**Job for tomorrow**
> Hi [name], tomorrow: **[property]**, checkout [time], next guests at [time]. [Special notes: hot tub / extra towels / host coming at 3pm.] Can you confirm? 👍

**Weekly pay**
> Hi [name], this week (to Fri [date]): [X] cleans = £[amount]. Paid today, ref [ref]. Thanks for all your work!

**Complaint from a host**
> Hi [name], the host at [property] mentioned [issue] (photo attached). Are you able to pop back today or tomorrow to sort it? It counts as a re-clean, as per our agreement. Let me know what happened so we can stop it happening again.
