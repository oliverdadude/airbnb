# COSHH Risk Assessment: Cleaning Products

**Business:** FreshTurn Property Care · **Assessed by:** [name] · **Date:** [date] · **Review by:** [date + 12 months, or when products change]

Based on the HSE's guidance for cleaning work. Keep the safety data sheet (SDS) for each product. Suppliers publish them free online.

| Product | Hazard (from label / SDS) | Who could be harmed | Controls | Extra action needed |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Multi-surface cleaner | Mild skin/eye irritant | Cleaners | Gloves; don't spray towards face; keep lid on | — |
| Bathroom cleaner (limescale) | Irritant; acidic | Cleaners | Gloves; ventilate bathroom (window/fan); never mix with bleach | — |
| Toilet cleaner / bleach | Corrosive; toxic gas if mixed with acids or ammonia | Cleaners, guests | Gloves; **never mix products**; ventilate; store upright in a locked cupboard away from guests | Tell all cleaners the "never mix" rule |
| Glass cleaner | Mild irritant | Cleaners | Use in ventilated rooms | — |
| Oven cleaner (deep cleans) | Corrosive | Cleaners | Gloves and eye protection; ventilate; follow label times | Deep cleans only |
| Dishwasher tablets / laundry pods | Harmful if swallowed; eye irritant | Guests (children) | Store high up or in a closed cupboard | — |

**General rules for every cleaner**
- Wear gloves for all chemical products.
- Never decant products into unlabelled bottles.
- Open a window or put the fan on when cleaning bathrooms.
- Wash hands after cleaning and before eating.
- Splash in the eyes: rinse with water for 15 minutes and get advice (NHS 111).
- Report any reaction (rash, breathing problems) to FreshTurn.
