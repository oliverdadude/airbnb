# Cleaning Service Agreement

This agreement is between **[Your name], trading as FreshTurn Property Care** ("FreshTurn") and **[Host name]** ("Host") for the property at **[address]**, starting **[date]**.

## 1. Services
FreshTurn will provide changeover cleaning after each guest checkout, including:
- a full clean following the FreshTurn turnover checklist and the Host's property notes
- washing and replacing bed linen and towels [using the Host's linen on site / using FreshTurn linen]
- restocking consumables and replacing small items (see section 4)
- a photo report after every clean, including any damage or missing items found

Other services (deep cleans, extra visits) are quoted separately.

## 2. Scheduling
2.1 The Host shares their booking calendar link (iCal) from Airbnb, Booking.com or similar. Cleans are scheduled automatically for each checkout.
2.2 Standard cleaning window: between **[11am]** checkout and **[4pm]** check-in. The Host will tell FreshTurn about any non-standard times.
2.3 Bookings added with less than **4 hours'** notice are charged as a rush clean (+25%).

## 4. Restocking & Replacements
4.1 **Consumables** (toilet roll, soap, bin bags, dishwasher tablets, tea and coffee, etc.) are [supplied by the Host and kept in a locked cupboard at the property / bought by FreshTurn and charged at cost + 10%]. FreshTurn tells the Host when the Host's own stock is running low.
4.2 **Broken, missing or worn-out items** (glasses, mugs, towels, bulbs, batteries, etc.): FreshTurn may replace items costing up to **£[20]** each without asking first, and charges them at cost + 10%. Anything above that is only bought after the Host approves it in writing (a text is fine).
4.3 Every replacement or restock appears as a separate line on the invoice, with the receipt attached.
4.4 **Guest damage:** FreshTurn sends the Host timestamped photos the same day. Claiming the cost back from the guest (through Airbnb, Booking.com or a deposit) is the Host's responsibility. The Host still pays FreshTurn for any replacement it has bought.
4.5 Large purchases (over **£[50]**) are paid for by the Host directly or paid in advance.

## 5. Payment
5.1 FreshTurn invoices **[monthly / every two weeks]**. Invoices are due within **7 days**.
5.2 The Host keeps a card on file, which is charged automatically on the due date [optional].
5.3 Cancellations with less than **24 hours'** notice are charged at **50%**. If the cleaner is already on site, the full price is charged.

## 6. Access
6.1 The Host provides access by [key safe / smart lock code / key]. Where possible, the Host provides a separate code for FreshTurn.
6.2 FreshTurn keeps all access details confidential and handles personal data in line with UK GDPR.

## 7. Quality Guarantee
If the Host or their guest reports a cleaning problem within **24 hours** of a clean, FreshTurn will return and fix it free of charge.

## 8. Damage & Responsibility
8.1 FreshTurn reports any guest damage it finds, with photos, but is not responsible for damage caused by guests.
8.2 If FreshTurn's cleaner accidentally damages the property, FreshTurn will repair or reimburse it, up to **£[___]** per incident, beyond which its public liability insurance applies.
8.3 FreshTurn is not responsible for normal wear and tear, or for items left in locked owner cupboards that FreshTurn was not asked to clean.

## 9. Ending the Agreement
Either party can end this agreement with **14 days'** written notice. Cleans already scheduled in that period will be completed and charged.
