# Cleaning Service Agreement

> Template only, not legal advice. Have a local lawyer review it before use. Fill in everything in [brackets].

This agreement is between **[FreshTurn Property Care LLC]** ("FreshTurn") and **[Host name]** ("Host") for the property at **[address]**, starting **[date]**.

## 1. Services
FreshTurn will provide turnover cleaning after each guest checkout, including:
- a full clean following the FreshTurn turnover checklist and the Host's property notes
- washing and replacing bed linens and towels [using the Host's linens on site / using FreshTurn linens]
- restocking consumables, billed at cost + 10%
- a photo report after every clean, including any damage or missing items found

Other services (deep cleans, extra visits) are quoted separately.

## 2. Scheduling
2.1 The Host shares their booking calendar link (iCal). Cleans are scheduled automatically for each checkout.
2.2 Standard cleaning window: between **[11:00 AM]** checkout and **[4:00 PM]** check-in. The Host will tell FreshTurn about any non-standard times.
2.3 Bookings added with less than **4 hours'** notice are charged as a rush clean (+25%).

## 4. Payment
4.1 FreshTurn invoices **[monthly / every two weeks]**. Invoices are due within **7 days**.
4.2 The Host keeps a card on file, which is charged automatically on the due date [optional].
4.3 Cancellations with less than **24 hours'** notice are charged at **50%**. If the cleaner is already on site, the full price is charged.

## 5. Access
5.1 The Host provides access by [smart lock code / lockbox / key]. Where possible, the Host provides a separate code for FreshTurn.
5.2 FreshTurn keeps all access details confidential.

## 6. Quality Guarantee
If the Host or their guest reports a cleaning problem within **24 hours** of a clean, FreshTurn will return and fix it free of charge.

## 7. Damage & Responsibility
7.1 FreshTurn reports any guest damage it finds, with photos, but is not responsible for damage caused by guests.
7.2 If FreshTurn's cleaner accidentally damages the property, FreshTurn will repair or reimburse it, up to **$[___]** per incident, beyond which its liability insurance applies.
7.3 FreshTurn is not responsible for normal wear and tear, or for items left unsecured in owner closets that FreshTurn was not asked to clean.

## 8. Ending the Agreement
Either party can end this agreement with **14 days'** written notice. Cleans already scheduled in that period will be completed and charged.
