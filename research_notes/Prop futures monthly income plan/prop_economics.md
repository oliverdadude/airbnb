Method note: The egress proxy for this session blocked direct page fetches (help.tradeify.co, tradeify.co, proptradingvibes.com, tradetanto.com, track360.io all refused). Every finding below comes from web-search result summaries and snippets, not full-page reads. ## 1. Disclosed statistics: pass rates, funded-to-payout conversion, payout size, repeat payouts

### Takeaway
Roughly 15-20% of evaluation accounts pass. About 30-45% of funded traders ever receive a payout. The combined result is that only about 5-7% of evaluation buyers are ever paid. Typical single payouts run about $1,000-$2,000. No source publishes how often funded traders get paid a second or third time, which is the statistic that matters most for monthly income.

### Cited Findings
**Tradeify (firm-disclosed, via its Funded Trader Agreement or plan pages):**
- For August 2025 to July 2026, across Growth, Advanced and Select evaluations: "17.2% of evaluation accounts initiated were successfully completed and afforded the opportunity to advance to the Funded Level." The snippet was cut off before the payout-rate figure. — [Tradeify Funded Trader Agreement](https://tradeify.co/funded-trader-agreement)
- An older Select plan page (about 300 days old, period not visible) says "28.5% of individual participants at the Funded Level received a payout." — [Tradeify SELECT Plan](https://tradeify.co/select-plan)
- Units differ: the 17.2% counts evaluation accounts and the 28.5% counts individuals. They cannot be multiplied together cleanly.

**Topstep (firm-reported, cited by third parties):**
- 2025 cohort: 16.8% of Trading Combines started in 2025 were passed. 51.8% of individual participants advanced at least once. 33.3% of individuals at the Funded Level received a payout. 0.71% of XFA traders advanced to a Live Funded Account. The figures are self-reported and not audited. — [quantvps prop firm statistics](https://www.quantvps.com/blog/prop-firm-statistics); [propdatalab Topstep](https://propdatalab.com/firms/topstep/); [fortunly](https://fortunly.com/statistics/prop-firm-challenge-pass-rate-statistics/)
- The 51.8% "advanced at least once" against the 16.8% per-Combine pass rate shows that per-person success is much higher than per-account success, because people buy many attempts. — same sources

**MyFundedFutures (plan-page disclaimer, per a third-party review):**
- 19.97% of evaluation participants advanced past the evaluation. 39.2% of participants who reached the simulated Funded Stage earned at least one payout. The measurement period is not stated. — [eltraderfinanciado MFFU review](https://www.eltraderfinanciado.com/en/review/myfundedfutures)

**Apex:**
- Apex publishes no pass rate or payout rate, only dollar totals. One source says the dashboard showed more than $828M in cumulative payouts since 2022, as of October 2026. Other sites give figures from $676M to $850M. — [traderpayout Apex vs MFFU](https://traderpayout.com/apex/apex-trader-funding-vs-myfundedfutures); [thepropjournalist Apex](https://thepropjournalist.com/reviews/apex-trader-funding/)

**Cross-industry vendor data (mostly forex/CFD firms, not US futures):**
- FPFX Tech covered about 300,000 accounts from about 100,000 traders at 10 unnamed firms (Finance Magnates, Sept 2024). 14% of traders passed. About 45% of those reached a payout, which is roughly 7% of all traders (14% x 45% is actually 6.3%). The average payout was about 4% of account size. The average trader spent about $800 over roughly 3 challenges. — [Finance Magnates](https://financemagnates.com/forex/analysis/exclusive-only-7-of-300000-prop-trading-accounts-achieved-payouts)
- The Funded Trader's CEO said about 20% of funded traders get paid, which is 1-2% of all clients. — [Finance Magnates](https://www.financemagnates.com/forex/only-1-in-20-traders-pass-prop-firm-challenges-reports-the-funded-trader/)

**Payout size (community and aggregator data):**
- PropFirmMatch futures tracker for Tradeify: average payout about $1,829, largest $12,500, median processing time about 3 hours. An earlier snapshot showed a $1,745 average and a $10,000 largest. PropFirmMatch publishes the average, not the median amount. — [PropFirmMatch futures payouts](https://propfirmmatch.com/futures/payouts); [PropFirmMatch payouts](https://propfirmmatch.com/payouts)
- PropChamps lists Tradeify at about a $3,000 average and Apex at about $2,000. Its methodology differs from PropFirmMatch and the figures conflict. — [PropChamps](https://propchamps.net/payouts)
- Payout Junction, across all firms for October 2026 to date: median payout $1,042, average $1,349. — [Payout Junction monthly](https://payoutjunction.com/monthly)
- These trackers rely on self-reported or community-submitted data. Firms do not publish how many accounts get paid each month. — [mytradingbuddy](https://mytradingbuddy.ai/blog/payout-junction-vs-propfirmmatch)

### Inferences
- Combining an evaluation pass rate of about 17% with a funded-to-paid rate of about 28-39% gives roughly 5-7% of evaluation accounts ever producing at least one payout. Several independent sources agree on this range.
- An average payout of $1,000-$2,000 is close to the post-Sept-2026 Select Flex caps ($1,250-$4,500), so most paid traders probably take one or two capped or sub-cap payouts rather than large ones.
- Consistent monthly income needs repeat payouts. No firm discloses the distribution of payouts per funded account, so any income plan built on several payouts per account per month has no empirical support.

### Gaps
- No source gives repeat-payout rates (the share of funded accounts with 2, 3 or 5+ payouts) for any firm.
- Tradeify's August 2025 to July 2026 payout-rate figure was cut off in the snippet.
- No source gives median payout amounts by firm. No academic study of US futures prop firms was found.

## 2. Tradeify Select rules in 2026 (25K / 50K / 100K / 150K)

### Takeaway
Select is a one-time-fee evaluation with no activation fee, a 40% consistency rule during the evaluation only, and EOD trailing drawdown. After passing, the trader picks a funded account. Select Flex pays every 5 winning days, up to 50% of profit, with caps. Select Daily allows daily payouts above a buffer and has a daily loss limit. Accounts bought after 1 Sept 2026 have lower payout caps.

### Cited Findings
**Evaluation (Select):**
| Size | Price (list, 40% consistency version) | Profit target | EOD trailing DD | Max contracts (minis/micros) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 25K | $109 | $1,500 | $1,000 | 1 / 10 |
| 50K | $165 | $3,000 | $2,000 | 4 / 40 |
| 100K | $265 | $6,000 | $3,000 | 8 / 80 |
| 150K | $369 | $9,000 | $4,500 | 12 / 120 |
- Prices — [quantvps Tradeify review](https://www.quantvps.com/blog/tradeify-review-and-payout-rules); range "$109-$369" — [Tradeify help: Which plan is right for you](https://help.tradeify.co/en/articles/10468265-which-plan-is-right-for-you). Conflicts: a tradeify.co comparison page shows 50K at $107 as of 15 July 2026, probably a promotion ([Tradeify blog](https://tradeify.co/post/best-affordable-futures-prop-firms-of-2026)). An older TradersPost post quotes $159 ([TradersPost](https://blog.traderspost.io/article/tradeify-pricing-evaluation-guide)). One source wrongly describes Select as a monthly subscription at $359 for 150K. Tradeify runs discount codes most months ([Phidias](https://phidiaspropfirm.com/education/tradeify-select)).
- An optional 50% evaluation-consistency add-on is available at a higher price. — [quantvps](https://www.quantvps.com/blog/tradeify-review-and-payout-rules)
- Reset fees (default Select): $75 / $109 / $169 / $239. — [quantvps](https://www.quantvps.com/blog/tradeify-review-and-payout-rules)
- No activation fee (help article dated 5 June 2026). — [Tradeify help: activation fees](https://help.tradeify.co/en/articles/10468246-are-there-activation-fees)
- Targets, drawdowns and contract limits — [Tradeify help: Select Evaluation Accounts](https://help.tradeify.co/en/articles/12853921-select-evaluation-accounts); [SaveOnPropFirms](https://saveonpropfirms.com/blog/tradeify-select-guide); [Phidias](https://phidiaspropfirm.com/education/tradeify-select). Some third parties list the 100K drawdown differently. The search summary treated the official help center's $3,000 as authoritative.
- The evaluation has no daily loss limit. To pass, the best day must be at most 40% of total profit. — [Tradeify pricing reference](https://help.tradeify.co/en/articles/14369021-tradeify-pricing-reference); [SaveOnPropFirms](https://saveonpropfirms.com/blog/tradeify-select-guide)
- The microscalping rule was removed from the evaluation in July 2026. On funded accounts it still applies: more than 50% of trades and profit must come from positions held over 10 seconds, and a breach blocks payouts but does not close the account. — [eltraderfinanciado news](https://www.eltraderfinanciado.com/en/news/tradeify/tradeify-removes-microscalping-rule-evaluation-jul-2026)

**Funded drawdown lock:**
- The trailing drawdown stops trailing at starting balance + $100 once the EOD balance exceeds starting balance + drawdown + $100. Example: a 50K account with an EOD balance of $52,100 locks its floor at $50,100. — [TradersPost](https://blog.traderspost.io/article/how-to-get-funded-with-tradeify); [fundedsim](https://fundedsim.com/en/prop-firms/tradeify). Conflict: one guide says it locks once the balance clears the level or when a payout is requested ([copilink](https://copilink.com/prop-firms/tradeify)). Another guide is about 280 days old and may predate current rules.
- Implied lock thresholds (EOD balance): 25K $26,100, 50K $52,100, 100K $103,100, 150K $154,600 (my calculation from the formula).

**Select Flex (funded):**
- Profit split 90/10. No daily loss limit. No funded consistency rule. No buffer. — [Tradeify pricing reference](https://help.tradeify.co/en/articles/14369021-tradeify-pricing-reference); [copilink](https://copilink.com/prop-firms/tradeify)
- A payout becomes available after 5 winning days, which need not be consecutive. The counter resets after each payout. Winning-day thresholds: more than $100 (25K), $150 (50K), $200 (100K), $250 (150K) net profit. Sources disagree on whether the threshold is ">" or ">=". — [mytradingbuddy](https://mytradingbuddy.ai/blog/tradeify-payout-rules); [homedadpro](https://homedadpro.com/en/tradeify-daily-vs-flex/); [Tradeify help: Select Flex & Daily payout policies](https://help.tradeify.co/en/articles/12853966-select-flex-and-select-daily-payout-policies)
- Each request is capped at 50% of total profit (balance minus starting balance). Minimum request $250. — [mytradingbuddy](https://mytradingbuddy.ai/blog/tradeify-payout-rules)
- Per-payout caps for accounts bought **after 1 Sept 2026**: $1,250 / $2,500 / $3,500 / $4,500. For accounts bought **before**: $1,250 / $3,000 / $4,000 / $5,000. — [Tradeify help: Select Evaluation Accounts](https://help.tradeify.co/en/articles/12853921-select-evaluation-accounts); [propfirmtrader](https://propfirmtrader.com/prop-firm/tradeify). Many third-party guides still show the older caps, including an August 2026 German Proptradingvibes guide.
- Policy text says "before/after 1 September 2026" without saying which side a purchase on that day falls. — [propfirmtrader](https://propfirmtrader.com/prop-firm/tradeify)
- Processing typically takes about 24h, or up to 72h outside business hours. — [completetradersedge](https://completetradersedge.com/tradeify-review-2026/)

**Select Daily (funded):**
- Daily loss limit: $500 / $1,000 / $1,250 / $1,750. — [Tradeify help: Daily Loss Limit](https://help.tradeify.co/en/articles/10468321-rules-daily-loss-limit); [Phidias](https://phidiaspropfirm.com/education/tradeify-select)
- Payouts can be requested daily, but only on profit above a buffer of starting balance + drawdown + $100. Buffers: 50K $2,100, 100K $2,600 (as reported, which does not match the $3,000 drawdown), 150K $3,600 (as reported, which does not match the $4,500 drawdown). Minimum $250. — [fundedsim](https://fundedsim.com/en/prop-firms/tradeify); [TradersPost](https://blog.traderspost.io/article/how-to-get-funded-with-tradeify). The 100K and 150K buffer figures conflict with the drawdown table and may come from an older version.
- Daily caps for accounts bought after 1 Sept 2026: $600 / $1,250 / $1,750 / $2,500. Before: $600 / $1,000 / $1,500 / $2,500. — [Tradeify help: Select Evaluation Accounts](https://help.tradeify.co/en/articles/12853921-select-evaluation-accounts)

**Lifetime cap:** No source mentioned a lifetime payout cap on Select sim-funded accounts. The live transition (below) acts as the de facto ceiling on sim payouts.

**Account limits:**
- Maximum 5 sim funded accounts at once, in any mix of Growth, Select and Lightning. The limit is per household. Tradeify's own example says two household members with 3 each (6 total) breaks the rule. Failed or expired accounts do not count. Passed evaluations can wait until a funded slot opens. Tradeify does not publish its definition of a household. — [Phidias: how many accounts](https://phidiaspropfirm.com/education/how-many-tradeify-accounts); [Proptradingvibes multiple accounts](https://proptradingvibes.com/blog/tradeify-multiple-accounts)
- Select evaluations: maximum 15 per rolling 30 days. Growth evaluations are unlimited. — same sources

**Copy trading and automation:**
- Copiers are allowed only between accounts the trader owns and manages, with all accounts trading the same direction. An inverted copier, or a leader flattened while followers stay open, counts as hedging. Copying other traders is prohibited. — [Phidias copy trading](https://phidiaspropfirm.com/education/tradeify-copy-trading); [CrossTrade](https://crosstrade.io/prop-firms/tradeify)
- Bots are allowed if the trader is the sole owner. The same bot may not be used across multiple firms. HFT bots are banned. — [Phidias copy trading](https://phidiaspropfirm.com/education/tradeify-copy-trading)
- Hedging (long in one account, short in another) puts every involved account in violation, forfeits profits from that period, and can lead to a permanent ban. — [Tradeify help: Hedging & Correlated Products](https://help.tradeify.co/en/articles/10495868-rules-hedging-trading-micros-or-minis)

**Sim to live (Elite Live):**
- A trader becomes eligible for review after 3 payouts on one account, or 10 total payouts since the last live transition across all plans. These are internal benchmarks: Tradeify may move a trader earlier, later, or never. Third-party sites citing "5 payouts" (earlier 8) appear outdated. — [Tradeify help: Elite Program](https://help.tradeify.co/en/articles/12969284-tradeify-elite-program); [Funded Trader Agreement](https://tradeify.co/funded-trader-agreement); [Tradeify help: New Select plan & live program changes](https://help.tradeify.co/en/articles/12987441-introducing-the-new-select-plan-changes-to-the-live-program)
- The transition is mandatory and closes all sim funded and evaluation accounts. Only accounts with at least one payout convert. Elite Live starts at a $0 balance with EOD trailing drawdown and no DLL. Profit split is 80/20. Payouts can be requested daily for any amount above $0. Profit made after the last payout is lost. Sim and live accounts cannot run together anywhere in the household. — [Proptradingvibes live](https://proptradingvibes.com/blog/tradeify-live-funded-accounts); [Phidias live](https://phidiaspropfirm.com/education/tradeify-live-account)
- A reward pool of $2,000-$15,000 on Elite Live is reported by one secondary source only (unverified). — [Phidias live](https://phidiaspropfirm.com/education/tradeify-live-account)

### Inferences
- Under the 50% rule, a 50K Flex account bought after 1 Sept 2026 needs at least $5,000 profit above start before a single request can reach the $2,500 cap. Smaller balances limit the request to half the profit. The trader nets 90%, so a capped 50K payout pays $2,250.
- Five successful accounts at the 50K cap would produce at most about $11,250 per payout cycle. But reaching 10 total payouts can trigger the live transition, which closes every sim account and restarts at a $0 balance. That puts a structural ceiling on long-run sim income.
- The 1 Sept 2026 cap cut (50K Flex $3,000 to $2,500, -17%) reduces the value of each payout. Planning must use the new caps.

### Gaps
- Whether a Flex payout withdrawal before the drawdown lock pulls the balance closer to the floor (it almost certainly does, but the exact mechanics are unconfirmed).
- The exact Select Daily buffer for 25K, and the conflict between the reported 100K/150K buffers and the drawdowns.
- The exact Select Flex funded max-contract scaling, if it differs from the evaluation.
- The 300K Select (limited release, V2 from 27 Aug 2026 with a $4,000 DLL — [Tradeify help: 300K Select](https://help.tradeify.co/en/articles/16497699-300k-select-account)) is out of scope.

## 3. Best account size for micro Nasdaq (MNQ) at 2026 volatility, and cost per expected payout

### Takeaway
The 50K offers the best value. Its profit target is only 1.5x its drawdown (the same as 25K, versus 2.0x for 100K and 150K), and it has the highest payout cap per evaluation dollar under the new caps. At the high MNQ volatility seen in 2026, the 25K's $1,000 drawdown fits only about 2-3 normal-sized stops, which is too tight.

### Cited Findings
- MNQ: tick 0.25 point = $0.50, so $2 per point. — [quantvps tick cheatsheet](https://www.quantvps.com/blog/futures-tick-cheatsheet)
- NQ 50-day average daily range about 721 points (2.44%) on the Sep '26 contract, a snapshot about 197 days old. — [Barchart NQ](https://www.barchart.com/futures/quotes/NQ*0/technical-analysis)
- Regime guide: NQ daily range of 200-300 points at VIX 12-15, 300-450 at VIX 15-20, 450-700 at VIX 20-30. — [Volatility Box](https://volatilitybox.com/research/nq-futures-volatility/)
- Recent October 2026 daily high-low ranges on MNQ Dec '26 were roughly 300-500 points on quiet days and 800-960 on volatile days (14, 16 and 22 Oct), based on the search tool's calculation from Investing.com's table, whose date stamps were inconsistent. — [Investing.com MNQ historical](https://www.investing.com/indices/micro-nasdaq-100-futures-historical-data)
- A broker glossary rule of thumb is a 200-400 point MNQ range ($400-$800 per contract), now probably understated. — [damnpropfirms MNQ](https://damnpropfirms.com/glossary/mnq-futures/)
- LuxAlgo models a 33.5% per-attempt pass rate for a "marginal" trader on Select 50K. This is a simulation, not observed data. — [LuxAlgo Tradeify](https://www.luxalgo.com/prop-firms/tradeify/)

### Inferences (my calculations from the cited rule figures)
| Size | DD / price | Target / DD | New Flex cap / price | DD per max micro | DD in 1-MNQ stops of 50 pts ($100) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 25K | 9.2x | 1.5 | 11.5x | $100 | 10 |
| 50K | 12.1x | 1.5 | 15.2x | $50 | 20 |
| 100K | 11.3x | 2.0 | 13.2x | $37.5 | 30 |
| 150K | 12.2x | 2.0 | 12.2x | $37.5 | 45 |
- With a 500-800 point daily range, a reasonable intraday MNQ stop is about 40-80 points, or $80-$160 per micro. Risking 1 micro per trade gives a 25K about 6-12 stop-outs of room and a 50K about 12-25. Running at the 50K's maximum (40 micros) would hit the drawdown in under one 25-point move, so the contract limits are far above what volatility allows. Drawdown, not contract limits, is the real constraint.
- Cost per expected payout (illustrative only). Using Tradeify's 17.2% evaluation-account completion rate and the older 28.5% funded-to-payout rate gives about 4.9% of evaluations reaching at least one payout. That is roughly 1 in 20 evaluations, about $3,400 in fees per first payout at the 50K list price of $165, or about $2,200 at $109 for 25K. This ignores resets and discounts, and mixes accounts with individuals. The FPFX average of about $800 spent per trader over about 3 attempts, with a 7% payout rate, gives a similar order of magnitude. A trader with a genuine edge will do much better than these population averages, but no data shows what that edge is worth.
- At the population level, the expected value is negative: an average payout of about $1,800 against roughly $2,000-$3,400 in fees per first payout. Being profitable requires being clearly above average, or getting multiple payouts per funded account.

### Gaps
- No measured 2026 MNQ ATR from a verified live feed. The October 2026 ranges come from a single table with inconsistent dates.
- No Tradeify pass rates by account size.

## 4. Pitfalls when scaling to several accounts

### Takeaway
Copying the same strategy across 5 Tradeify accounts is allowed (same owner, same direction). But it gives no diversification: one bad day hits every account at once. Hedging or copier errors get every linked account flagged. Payout reviews can deny a payout and disable the account. The live-transition rule caps how long a multi-account sim operation can run.

### Cited Findings
- Hedging across accounts (including copier glitches where the leader is flat and followers stay open) puts all involved accounts in violation, forfeits profits and can bring a permanent ban. — [Tradeify help: Hedging](https://help.tradeify.co/en/articles/10495868-rules-hedging-trading-micros-or-minis); [Phidias](https://phidiaspropfirm.com/education/tradeify-copy-trading)
- A failed payout verification denies the payout and disables the account (Level Up FAQ, which describes the standard review process). — [Tradeify help: Level Up FAQ](https://help.tradeify.co/en/articles/17281277-level-up-faq)
- The funded microscalping rule (more than 50% of trades and profit from holds over 10s) can block payouts. — [eltraderfinanciado](https://www.eltraderfinanciado.com/en/news/tradeify/tradeify-removes-microscalping-rule-evaluation-jul-2026)
- The 40% consistency rule applies only to the Select evaluation. Flex and Daily funded accounts have no consistency rule, but Flex's 5-winning-day threshold and 50% request cap act as implicit consistency filters. — [Tradeify pricing reference](https://help.tradeify.co/en/articles/14369021-tradeify-pricing-reference); [mytradingbuddy](https://mytradingbuddy.ai/blog/tradeify-payout-rules)
- The household cap means a spouse or partner cannot add 5 more accounts. — [Phidias](https://phidiaspropfirm.com/education/how-many-tradeify-accounts)
- A bot may not be run across multiple firms, which limits spreading one automated strategy across Tradeify and other firms. — [Phidias copy trading](https://phidiaspropfirm.com/education/tradeify-copy-trading)
- The live transition is discretionary and mandatory. It closes all sim accounts and evaluations, and progress since the last payout is lost. — [Tradeify help: Elite Program](https://help.tradeify.co/en/articles/12969284-tradeify-elite-program)
- Third-party Trustpilot reviews of other firms (DNA Funded, HFT Funded) report accounts closed for alleged hedging after payout requests. This is weak, unverified evidence and does not involve Tradeify. — [Trustpilot DNA Funded](https://pt.trustpilot.com/review/dnafunded.com)

### Inferences
- Five copied accounts behave like one account at 5x size. A single drawdown-breaking day can wipe out all five, costing 5 evaluations (about $825 at the 50K list price) plus the time to re-qualify. Staggering accounts across stages, or varying size per account, reduces the chance that everything fails together. This is a practitioner tactic, not a sourced rule.
- Scaling income through more accounts runs into two limits: 5 per household and 15 Select evaluations per 30 days. In practice, the maximum sim income is 5 accounts x cap x 90% per Flex cycle (at least 5 trading days), until the live transition resets everything.

### Gaps
- No Tradeify-specific data on payout denial rates, or on how often multi-account (copied) traders are flagged.
- No published rule that explicitly bans one person running an identical strategy across their own 5 accounts. The sources reviewed show it is allowed.
- No disclosure of how many funded traders run multiple accounts, or of their outcomes.
