# Research: Airbnb Cleaning in Bath (October 2026)

What I found, how sure I am, and where it came from. **Check the items marked ⚠️ yourself** before relying on them; the sources were thin or conflicting.

| Finding | Confidence | Source |
|---|---|---|

**What this means:** there are hundreds of potential clients within a short drive, and guests paying about £190 a night expect hotel-standard cleaning.

## 4. Damage claims

| Finding | Confidence | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Airbnb: request payment through the Resolution Centre **within 14 days of checkout**, then escalate to AirCover | Medium ⚠️ (one source says "or before next check-in") | [Hostfully](https://www.hostfully.com/blog/how-to-file-airbnb-aircover-claim/), [RedAwning](https://www.redawning.com/pm/post/airbnb-aircover-claims-property-managers) |
| Airbnb host damage protection doesn't cover normal wear and tear | High | [Hostfully](https://www.hostfully.com/blog/airbnb-damage-policy/) |
| Booking.com: hosts can request a damage fee **up to 14 days after checkout** | High | [Booking.com damage policy](https://www.booking.com/content/damage-policy.html), [Holidu](https://www.holidu.co.uk/magazine/how-to-claim-damages-on-booking) |

**What this means:** photograph damage the same day and tell the host immediately. The spreadsheet's Damage tab counts down the 14 days.

## 6. Short-term let rules

| Finding | Confidence | Source |
|---|---|---|
| England's national short-term let **register is not live yet**; no portal or fee published | Medium | [GuestReady](https://www.guestready.com/blog/englands-short-term-rental-registration-scheme/), [Fox Davidson](https://www.foxdavidson.co.uk/2026/04/28/holiday-let-regulations-2026/) |
| Reported target: **fully running by March 2027** | ⚠️ Low (one source) | [Drake Mortgages](https://www.drakemortgages.co.uk/knowledge-articles/englands-short-let-register/) |
| B&NES council has pushed for stronger short-let controls (like London's) | Medium | [B&NES decision record](https://democracy.bathnes.gov.uk/ieDecisionDetails.aspx?ID=1114) |

**What this means:** registration probably won't hurt you much, because the hosts who stay will be the more professional ones you want. Tighter local planning rules could reduce listings, so also offer end-of-tenancy and serviced-apartment cleans as a backup.

## 7. Rubbish

| Finding | Confidence | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Holiday lets generally **can't use household council bin collections** (Controlled Waste Regulations 2012); guest rubbish counts as commercial waste | Medium (other councils state it; national rule) | [Conwy Council](https://www.conwy.gov.uk/en/Business/Holiday-Let-Recycling-and-Waste-Collections.aspx) |
| Central Bath has rules on business waste: not left on streets 10am–6pm, labelled containers | Medium | [B&NES decision](https://democracy.bathnes.gov.uk/documents/s87986/E3620%20Decision%20-%20Business%20Waste%20Collection%20Changes%20in%20Central%20Bath.pdf), [B&NES newsroom](https://newsroom.bathnes.gov.uk/news/waste-and-recycling?page=0) |

**What this means:** ask every host how their rubbish is collected and follow it exactly. It's their responsibility, but a fine or a complaint lands on you if cleaners get it wrong.
