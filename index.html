FAQ

Questions hosts ask us

Do you manage my Airbnb listing or talk to guests?

No. We're a property care company, not a property manager. You keep full control of your listing, pricing and guest communication. We take care of everything physical: cleaning, linens, supplies and upkeep.

How do you know when to clean?

You share your Airbnb, VRBO or Booking.com calendar link (iCal). New bookings automatically appear in our schedule and a clean is booked for checkout day. You can also add or move cleans manually.

What if a guest damages something?

We photograph every room after checkout and send you a damage report with timestamped photos, which is exactly what Airbnb's AirCover claim process asks for.

Are your cleaners insured and background-checked?

Yes. Every team member is background-checked and trained on our checklist, and we carry general liability insurance and a janitorial bond.

What about same-day turnovers?

They're our bread and butter. With a standard 11 AM checkout and 4 PM check-in, we have plenty of time. Rush cleans with less than 4 hours' notice carry a 25% surcharge.

How does billing work?

One itemized invoice at the end of each month, payable by card or bank transfer. No contracts. Cancel any time with 14 days' notice.
