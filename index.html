FAQ

Questions hosts ask us

Something else? Ask us in the walkthrough form above.

Which areas do you cover?

Bath and the surrounding villages, roughly within 20 minutes of the city centre. Further out? Ask us; we can usually help for a small travel charge.

Do you manage my Airbnb listing or talk to guests?

No. We're a cleaning and property care company, not a property manager. You keep full control of your listing, pricing and guest messages. We take care of everything physical: cleaning, linens, supplies and upkeep.

How do you know when to clean?

You share your Airbnb, Booking.com or Vrbo calendar link. New bookings appear in our schedule automatically and a clean is booked for checkout day. You can also add or move cleans yourself.

What if a guest damages something?

We photograph every room after checkout and send you a damage report with timestamped photos, which is the evidence Airbnb's AirCover claim process asks for.

Who are your cleaners?

An experienced local team we know and trust personally. Each property gets a main cleaner plus a backup who knows it, so a clean is never missed. Everyone is trained on our checklist, and we carry general liability insurance.

What about same-day turnovers?

They're most of what we do. With a standard 11am checkout and 4pm check-in there's plenty of time. Cleans booked with less than 4 hours' notice carry a 25% rush fee.

How does billing work?

One itemised invoice at the end of each month, payable by card or bank transfer. No contracts. Cancel any time with 14 days' notice.
